# SIGIDI mark concepts: round 1

These images are monochrome exploration sheets. They are not production assets or vector masters.

## Prompt set

All four prompts used the approved SIGIDI constraints:

- A minimal geometric stick-figure automaton in an axe-strike posture.
- One broad triangular or wedge-shaped blade with a narrow tang and plain shaft.
- Flat black marks on a white background.
- No face, costume, wordmark, color, texture, shadow, or three-dimensional treatment.
- No double-headed axe, thunderbolt, crown, Shango imagery, depiction of Ogun, or unverified sacred symbol.
- Three substantially different iterations in each family.

### Family A: Kinetic Scaffold

Explore three strike phases with modular bars and circular joints: overhead preparation, diagonal downswing, and low follow-through.

### Family B: Negative-Space Forge

Build the figure and axe through white counter-shapes cut into compact black silhouettes. Change the mass, counter-shape, and strike direction in each iteration.

### Family C: Forged Automaton

Make assembly the central idea through a small number of bars, pins, pivots, and fitted parts. Change the mechanism and silhouette in each iteration.

### Family D: Single-Stroke Force

Reduce the figure to one continuous or nearly continuous heavy path. Change the path, balance, and strike direction in each iteration.

## Review sequence

1. Compare all 12 marks in monochrome.
2. Select up to three marks for refinement.
3. Test the shortlist at app-icon and favicon sizes.
4. Apply the production palette and wordmark only after the structural shortlist is approved.

## Selected shortlist

- B3: Negative-Space Forge
- C2: Forged Automaton
- D1: Single-Stroke Force
